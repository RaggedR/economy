# Milestone 8 — one staple market

Sellers post, then households buy. The clear is one pure plan. Each leaf commits the lots it was handed. Starvation runs after that, and only for the people who bid. Milestones 0–7 are unchanged on `monthSeed`.

## What landed

`fill` is an effect: `{ listing, buyer, qty }`. A positive qty pays `price × qty` from the buyer's purse to the listing's seller, row `staple`, and takes that many units off the listing and off the firm's `staple` inventory. A zero qty journals the effect and moves nothing, listing included. The listing id may be empty when the plan had no lot. Applying a qty the listing or the inventory cannot cover throws `LeakError`.

`postGoodGate` posts one staple lot. The gate's listing id is empty; `applyEffect` mints it. Qty plus what that firm has already listed must fit in `inventory.staple`. A folded firm cannot post. Other goods are not posted.

The interface's payload union has no good, so a good listing carries `{ good: "staple" }` and nothing else. `goodOf` reads it. Ore, mine, and job payloads are unchanged.

`clearStaple` plans every bid before any fill commits. Lots are walked cheapest first, price then id. A buyer who named a seller takes only that seller's lots on this pass. A lot whose claimants want more than it holds is a sell-out: `nextIndex` permutes them, one draw per remaining claimant, and each receives one unit in that order while the lot lasts. One claimant, or a lot that covers every claim, does not draw. Cash and inventory cap the qty. The same seed replays.

A buyer whose fill is still zero, and who still has cash, gets one retry on whatever staple remains, at the posted price. The named seller does not bind that pass. Shares and mines are not sold. `params.distressDiscountPercent` is still unread.

The household shape is `buy`. `lots` is not an interface field. It is the plan, attached so the leaf does not clear. One command commits that buyer's lots under one correlation id. `runMutation` writes `rationed` when `filled` is 0 or `filled` is short of `asked`, and `ok` when a positive order is filled in full. A zero fill is in both logs. Reconciliation already treated `rationed` as landed.

Starvation is a mark, not a journal line, and not a leaf. A positive staple fill sets the counter to 0. A zero fill steps it, capped at 3. People who did not bid are left alone, so a month in which nobody buys food does not starve the town. Three foodless months and nothing liquid is `death`. Liquid means deposits, bank shares, private loans held, and undeposited mines. A house is not liquid. `death` sets `alive` to false and moves no asset. The firm, the job, and the house stay. `assertInvariant` does not require a dead person to hold nothing. Inheritance is a later milestone.

`runMonth` calls `runStaple` with no bids, after the loan check and before `remainderDraws`. No staple listing, or no bid: it writes nothing. `monthSeed` has no listing, so its journal is still `pay` then `lendBank`.

The stranger scene is `demo-out/market`, appended after the purse scene on the same id source and clock. The shop posts one staple at 10. Ada and Ben each ask for one. Cleo asks with an empty purse. Seed 1 gives the unit to Ben: his fill is `ok`, he pays 10, the shop holds 10, the listing and the inventory are empty. Ada and Cleo are `rationed` at 0. Starvation is ada 1, ben 0, cleo 1. Money supply stays 100. Interbank stays 0. Month stays 0. The post is `engine` and has no intent record. The three fills are `agent`.

## Rulings

- The allocation is computed once. A leaf that runs first cannot take the units the plan gave the next buyer. The tensor's left-before-right schedule is why the plan has to exist before either commit.
- A full fill is `ok`. A shortfall, including `filled: 0`, is `rationed`. Both commit. A refusal still does not.
- A named seller is not replaced by a cheaper lot. The retry is the only pass that may buy a different seller, and only after a zero fill, and only at the posted price, and only while the buyer still has cash.
- The distress sale of shares and mines is not this market. Deposits are already the means of payment.
- Starvation is the engine's rule after the clear. It is not a shape the household answers.
- A house does not keep a person alive. Deposits, shares, private loans held, and undeposited mines do. Death does not distribute them.
- `salesLastMonth` is not updated here. Tax on fills stays where the opening already reads `taxOwed`.
- The month prefix on `monthSeed` still does not increment `month`.
- `--live` still returns `not implemented`.
