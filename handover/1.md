# Milestone 1 — the books, and the four-step story

## What landed

`src/world/types.ts` types the world from Part II. `src/world/world.ts` is `applyEffect`. `src/world/gates.ts` returns `{ ok: true, effect } | { ok: false, reason }`. An `Ok` effect is legal, so the reducer does not judge it again. You cannot hold an illegal effect. `assertInvariant` throws `LeakError` on a leak. That throw is a bug, not a refusal.

`Bank` carries three counters so a reducer bug can fail the check. They are not a new instrument.

- `depositLiability` is negative, and equals minus the deposits held at that bank by people, firms, and the government.
- `loansOutstanding` equals the sum of that bank's loan principals.
- `sharesOutstanding` equals the units of that bank's share records.

`equity` stays the interface: interest and ore sales raise it, dividends and losses lower it. It is not the deposit liability.

`src/world/seed.ts` `storySeed()`: ada `{a: 1000, b: 0}`, ben `{a: 0, b: 800}`, government zero. Bank `a` has deposit liability −1000, equity −1000, rate 100. Bank `b` has deposit liability −800, equity −800, rate 105. No firms, houses, or mines. `month` 0, `nextId` 1, `rng` `{ seed: 1, stream: 0 }`.

`src/cli/econ.ts` is the one executable. JSON is the default. Exit 0 on success. A refusal prints `refused: ...` to stderr and exits 1. `run` throws `Refusal` so the harness can catch it. `main` uses `realIds` and `realClock`. The harness calls in-process `run` and passes `--corr`. The CLI does not mint a second id when `--corr` is present. One `commit()` closure: the reason is checked, then the effect is applied, then the world is saved, then one journal line with source `"agent"`. `commitEngine` is exported and is not an argv command, so a caller cannot forge `--source engine`.

The scripted story in `src/bridge/story.ts` goes through the teller registry (`pay`, `lend`, `repay`) into `demo-out/story`. Repay looks up the single loan id. The tests in `src/world/world_test.ts` and `src/bridge/harness_test.ts` pin this end state, minor units:

1. ada pays ben 200, row `wages`. Interbank stays 0. Each deposit liability is unchanged. Money supply stays 1800.
2. ada pays bank `b` 100, row `bankInterest`. Ada holds nothing at `b`, so the debit is at `a`. Interbank becomes −100. Bank `b` equity becomes −700. Bank `a` equity stays −1000. Money supply becomes 1700. The sum of deposit liabilities changes, because the deposit is destroyed into equity.
3. Bank `a` lends ada 300, collateral `"note"`, installment 30, months 10. `loansOutstanding` is 300. Money supply is 2000. Milestone 1 `lend` does not require the collateral to exist.
4. Repay 100. Principal is 200. Bank `a` equity stays −1000.
5. ada pays bank `a` 40, row `bankInterest`. Principal stays 200. Equity becomes −960. `loansOutstanding` stays 200. ada holds `{a: 860}`, ben holds `{a: 200, b: 800}`. Money supply is 1860. The sum of deposit liabilities is −1860.

A separate round trip lends 250 and repays it in full. Deposit liabilities, `loansOutstanding`, the money supply, and ada's deposit return to the opening. An interest `pay` of 25 between the lend and the repay leaves principal unchanged, raises equity by 25, and after the full repay the loan row is back at zero while equity stays at the interest-raised level. The money supply is down by 25.

Person-to-person, drawn from both banks: ben pays ada 300 from `b`, then ada pays ben 1100. End state ada `{a: 0, b: 200}`, ben `{a: 1000, b: 600}`, interbank 0, deposit liabilities still −1000 and −800. An equal balance is drawn from bank `a` first: ada `{a: 100, b: 100}` paying ben 50 ends `{a: 50, b: 100}`, interbank 0.

`src/world/books.ts` prints the five rows and the purses. `src/world/store.ts` loads a missing or bad file as `storySeed()` and saves pretty JSON.

## Rulings

- A pay whose payee is a bank, funded from the other bank, moves interbank and changes the sum of deposit liabilities. The deposit identity is that the sum of deposit liabilities equals minus the money supply, and destroying a deposit into equity changes that supply. A person-to-person pay, including one split across both banks, moves neither interbank nor the sum of deposit liabilities. The mechanics paragraph in `PLAN.md` is the rule. The one-line required test that says a cross-bank pay leaves the sum of deposit liabilities unchanged describes the person-to-person case. Do not freeze that sum on a pay to a bank.
- Interbank is the single antisymmetric claim of `a` on `b`. It pays no interest. It is not a loan the bank leaf decides. It moves only when the payee is a bank and the payer's balance at that bank is short. If the payee is `a` and the other bank is `b`, interbank rises by the amount. If the payee is `b` and the other bank is `a`, interbank falls by the amount.
- Person-to-person pay debits the payer's largest balance, ties broken by bank `a` before bank `b`, and credits the payee at that same bank. If the largest balance is short, the remainder is taken at the other bank and the payee is credited there for that portion. A total shortfall is a gate refusal. Applying an illegal pay throws `LeakError`. A bank id as `from` throws: a bank does not pay through `pay`.
- `repayBank` is funded like a pay to the lending bank (that bank's deposit first, then the other bank, same interbank rule) and equity does not rise. Principal and `loansOutstanding` fall. A same-bank repay lowers loans and raises deposit liability by the same amount, and equity is unchanged.
- `lendBank` credits the borrower at that bank, lowers deposit liability, raises `loansOutstanding`, and mints `loan-N` from `World.nextId`. Equity is unchanged.
- NFA of a person, a firm, or the government is deposits at `a` plus deposits at `b`, minus bank-loan principal owed, plus private loans held, minus private loans issued. NFA of a bank is `equity` only. Deposit liability and interbank are already inside that equity identity, and adding them again double-counts interest. A deposited mine is a real asset and is not in the money identity `equity === loansOutstanding + depositLiability + signedInterbank`. Share units are a share instrument, not money equity. Total NFA across holders is 0.
- `refPrice` returns `params.initialRef` and does not read sales. Milestones 0–5 record no independent unit sale.
- Flow identity is per-effect. Each effect's lines equal that holder's change in NFA for that effect, and each named row sums to 0 across holders, and then `assertInvariant` runs. It is not a full Godley capital-account matrix. `bankPrincipal` is in the `FlowRow` union and `repayBank` does not emit it. Lending and repayment record no income line, because ΔNFA is 0. `openingNfa` is a function, not a field of `World`.
- The Effect union in `types.ts` is the set this range applies: `pay`, `lendBank`, `repayBank`, `issuePrivate`, `transferPrivate`, `writeOff`, `post`, `depositMine`, `seize`, `dividend`, `setLoanRate`. Later tags are added when their milestone arrives. `draw` is not in the union.
- The CLI also exposes the milestone 2 commands, through the same `commit`. The month demo does not call them. See `handover/2.md`.
- `linesOf` stamps `month` onto the flow lines. It takes the built lines and the month. The effect is not a parameter.
