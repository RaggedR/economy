Inheritance is the prompt's resolved routing: secured collateral first, houses to the homeless in 10-unit pieces, liquid assets escheat to the government, the decedent's own unsecured debts are written off, and the first child inherits the net estate only.

## What landed

`fertility` returns `none`, `voluntary`, or `mandatory`. A person who owns nothing is `none`. Mandatory is 100 owned units, deposits of `10 × cashGift`, and one unliened quantum spare. Voluntary is `houseQuantum × (living children + 1)` spare and `3 × cashGift × (living children + 1)` in deposits. `cashGift` is 60, twelve months at `stapleReservePrice`. The cash test reads deposits. `fertilityWeight` names all five objectives at 0. `planBirths` throws when a voluntary person has a non-zero weight. A mandatory parent births once in the month. `birthGate` accepts only that band. The reducer does not judge the gate again.

`birth` mints the child, then the house. The child copies the parent's objective, with education all 0, no job, health 0, happiness 0. The gift is 10 units and 60 deposits, on the flow row `estate`. The source is an unliened house with a spare quantum: a house that is not the dwelling, then the lowest id. A house of exactly one quantum is reassigned to the child. A larger house is cut, and the new house is the child's dwelling.

`planEstate` takes the loans the decedent owes, in id order. A bank loan whose collateral still carries that lien is `seize`. Any other debt of theirs is `writeOff`. The heir is the first living entry of `children`. That heir receives each remaining house at its full unit count, then `vest` when anything else remains. `grantHouse.units` is the size of that completion. An heir who already has a dwelling does not move in. A childless estate gives `houseQuantum` of a lien-free house to one homeless person, and repeats while both remain. One homeless person does not draw. Several do. The index is chosen in the planner and stored on the effect. The world returned beside the effects differs from the input only in `rng`, and that `rng` is saved before the commits. A quantum with nobody homeless, and every liquid holding, escheats.

`escheat` and `vest` are one conveyance. Deposits move on row `estate`. Share holders are reassigned, and `sharesOutstanding` stays. A private receivable changes holder, with a `Δprivate` pair at price 0. An undeposited unliened mine changes owner. A deposited mine stays where it is. A leftover house changes owner, and its occupant is cleared when the occupant was the decedent. A remaining lien throws. Firms change owner. The decedent's `firm` becomes null. The recipient's `firm` is set only when it was null, to the lowest-id firm they now own.

`death` is last. It marks the person dead, clears the job and the dwelling, and clears any house occupant that was them. A job is not an asset. After that mark, `assertInvariant` requires the dead person to hold nothing: deposits, `firm`, houses, shares, private receivables, mines, firms. A death applied while any of those remain throws.

Starvation still fires on three foodless months and nothing liquid. A house is not liquid. The death now goes through `commitDeath`, so the house is routed.

The scheduler calls `runBirths`, then `settleDead`, after the remainder draws and before the auditor. `monthSeed` is under both fertility lines and nobody has died, so those calls append nothing.

## Rulings

- A voluntary weight of zero births nobody. A non-zero weight throws: this milestone does not roll it.
- The gift of cash is a deposit transfer. The distress sale is still unbuilt. `distressDiscountPercent` is unread here.
- The mandatory line requires a spare unliened quantum, so a fully liened estate cannot emit a house it cannot fund.
- The first living child inherits the net estate. A younger living child receives nothing from that death. A dead firstborn is skipped.
- One homeless recipient does not draw. The planner names the recipient, and the effect does not draw a second time.
- A dead person holds nothing once the death mark has run. The mark is the last effect of the plan. A bare death of someone who still holds an asset throws.
- A private receivable the decedent holds changes holder. It is not written off. Persons are not private-loan issuers. Secured collateral is seized before the free houses move. An unsecured bank debt of theirs, whose lien is already gone, is written off.
- `t₀` is `townSeed` plus `townCensus`. It is not `monthSeed`, and it does not run a month.
- Margin calls, the ordinary month's price posts, its debt and mine and share markets, and `orderMaterial` are outside this milestone.
- `--live` still returns `not implemented`.
