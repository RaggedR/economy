# Milestone 2 — the rest of the financial gates

## What landed

Each of these has a gate in `src/world/gates.ts` and a reducer case in `src/world/world.ts`. The illegal shape cannot be held. An empty reason never reaches the reducer.

- `issuePrivate`. Legal when the installment times the months is an integer, at least the principal, and both the principal and the installment are positive. `issuerKind` is `"firm" | "government"`. `issueDebt` on the CLI is the same gate. The record is `debt-N`. A private loan moves an existing deposit. It does not create money. There is no bond type beside private loans, and no equity instrument in a firm. The private-loan row is structurally zero from the one record. The accumulator in `instrumentSums` is named `privateNet`, because `private` is a reserved word.
- `transferPrivate`. The buyer pays `price` and receives the instrument. Flows are `debtTrade` (buyer −price, seller +price) and the private-loan principal moving the other way. A lien blocks the transfer.
- `writeOff`. Both sides of the instrument disappear. No deposit moves. The flow is `writeOff`: the asset holder loses the principal, the issuer gains it.
- `depositMine`. The parcel's owner becomes the bank, `depositedAt` is that bank, and `share-N` units are minted to the previous owner. No deposit moves. A lien blocks it. An already-deposited mine is refused. House units, where a house exists, are a positive multiple of 10. Mine stock is at least 0. A deposited mine's owner is a bank id.
- `dividend`. Floor pro-rata across that bank's shares. Equity falls by the amount paid. If the paid total is 0, the effect records no flow lines. Dust stays as equity. The dust test deposits a mine at `b`, pays ada's entire 1000 to bank `b` as interest, then replaces the shares with ada holding 1 and ben holding 2 against `sharesOutstanding` 3. Equity was 200. The floors are 66 and 133, paid is 199, dust equity is 1. ada's balance at `b` is 66 and ben's is 933. The sheet still closes.
- `seize`. The loan disappears, `loansOutstanding` falls by the principal, equity falls by the principal, `losses` rises by the principal, the collateral's owner becomes the bank, and the lien is cleared.
- `post`. The gate's listing id is `""`. `applyEffect` mints `list-N` through `takeId`. Two posts gated against one world before either is applied receive `list-1` and `list-2`.
- `setLoanRate`. An integer rate, which may be negative or zero. A non-integer is refused.

`sellCapital` is legal only when that bank's equity is negative, or its loans exceed `leverageMultiple` times its marked mine base. The over-ceiling fixture is `loansOutstanding: 1001` against a base of 1000 and a multiple of 10. A book with `loansOutstanding: 1` is inside the ceiling.

Mines and shares exist as data because `depositMine` needs them. The parcel count is whatever the tests need. It is not the t₀ population. That population is the seed of milestone 12.

The CLI commands, all through the same `commit`, are `pay`, `lend`, `repay`, `request-loan`, `post-rate`, `post-material`, `sell-capital`, `issue-private` / `issue-debt`, `transfer-private`, `write-off`, `deposit-mine`, `dividend`, `seize`. Amounts match `^-?\d+$`. A bank id is `a` or `b`. Reads are `books`, `reconcile` (needs `--intent`), `journal recent` (`--n`, default 10), and `journal trace`.

`src/world/params.ts` holds the knobs with no stated default: `ltvPercent` 50, `leverageMultiple` 10, `houseUnitValue` 100, `initialRef` `{ common: 10, advanced: 40 }`, `rateStep` 1. Reducers read this object. Sweeping the knobs is not part of the start.

## Rulings

- `setLoanRate` is in the Effect union so `postLoanRate` can change `Bank.loanRate`. The interface's Effect list does not name it.
- Listing ids are minted in `applyEffect`. The id on the gated listing is the empty string, and the reducer ignores it.
- A free house stores `lien: null`. The lien read is `house ? house.lien : mine ? mine.lien : null`. `house?.lien ?? mine?.lien` treats that null as missing, looks up a mine that is not there, and reports every free house as liened. The same ternary is in `requestLoanGate` and `seizeGate`.
- A zero marked base makes `sellCapital` distressed whenever loans are positive, because the test is `loans > multiple × base`. The rate policy's "full book" is a different predicate. See `handover/4.md`.
- `FlowRow` lines for these effects are the ones named above. `depositMine`, `post`, `setLoanRate`, and `issuePrivate` record no flow lines.
