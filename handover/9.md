# Milestone 9 — the special day

A month is special when `month % 12 === 0`. Month 0 is one. The day runs after staple and before the remainder draws. Sellers post, each clear is one pure plan, then each leaf commits only what that plan gave it. The dividend, the job archive, a failed rent's vacate, and `pinHealth` are the engine. Milestones 0–8 are unchanged on `monthSeed`.

## What landed

The new effects are `tradeShare`, `transferHouse`, `occupy`, `hire`, `deliverMachine`, `rent`, `vacate`, `pinHealth`, and `archiveJobs`. `dividend` was already an effect. `fill` stays staple-only. A zero qty on a share, a house, or a machine journals the effect and moves nothing. `hire` carries `seated` and `keep`. `rent` carries `pay`. `vacate` has no gate: the engine commits it.

Share listings carry `{ bank }`. House and rent listings carry `{ house }`. A holder can own both banks, and an owner can hold more than one house, so the payload is required. Job and machine payloads are unchanged.

`clearShares` walks one bank's lots cheapest first, price then id. There is no named seller and no retry. The buyer does not take their own lot. A contested lot draws from `RngState`, one unit per claimant per pass, and loops while the lot and the claims last. One claimant, or a lot that covers every claim, does not draw. `sharesOutstanding` does not change. A buyer with no record of that bank receives a new one. The row is `shareTrade`.

The dividend runs after that book, for bank a and then bank b, and only when the gate accepts: equity above zero and shares outstanding. Each holder of record is paid `floor(equity × units / sharesOutstanding)`. Dust stays equity. The credit is a new deposit, so equity and the deposit liability both fall by the amount paid. The row is `dividend`.

`clearHouses` is not an auction. A bidder who cannot pay the ask, and the seller, are not in the draw. One solvent bidder wins with no draw. Two or more are permuted, one draw per bidder including the last one left, and the first in that order wins the whole house. The plan spends a local cash map. The returned world differs only in `rng`. A won house pays `houseTrade`, clears the dwelling, and sets the owner. Qty is 1 or 0. A lien blocks the transfer. `occupy` then `let` moves the owner out only when they are the occupant. `occupy` moves the owner in and clears a third-party occupant.

`seatJobs` seats incumbents who `keep` first, in person-id order, at the salary they already hold. `keep` skips the education check and requires the person to hold that firm and career. `apply` always checks `education[career] >= generation`. Eligible applicants are taken by descending health. A shortfall of healthier applicants lets a low-health applicant in. A tie at one health, and only when the band is larger than the seats left, draws. A person who wins a second listing loses the earlier seat, and that chair is not put back for the rest of the day. An ineligible or unposted wish is committed with `seated: true`, so the gate refuses it. A legal loss is `seated: false`, which journals `hire` and moves nothing. After the hires, and only when a job was posted, the engine writes `archiveJobs` for `floor(month / 12)`, copying `payload.slots` from before the hires.

`clearMachines` is cheapest-first at or under the limit, for the named listing. The buyer is a firm and does not buy its own listing. Delivery pays `machinePurchase` and mints one `Machine` per unit: owner is the buyer, builder is the listing's seller, type and generation come from the payload. A generation enters the world at delivery. The firm's strategy is not this day. A tech firm is the only poster.

`seatRents` gives the house to the sitting occupant when they bid and can pay the posted year. Otherwise the highest salary wins, and a tie at one salary draws. The year is paid in full or not at all. Inability to pay is a refusal, including when the leaf sends `pay: false`, because the gate checks the purse first. A solvent loss commits `pay: false` and is `rationed`. A listed house that nobody wins, and that still has an occupant, is vacated by the engine. The refusal is already in the intent log and writes no journal line. `pinHealth` with cause `homeless` then runs for every living person whose dwelling is null, in id order.

`runMonth` calls `runSpecialDay(ctx, {})` after `runStaple` and before the remainder draws. A month that is not a multiple of 12 returns before any write, even when the orders are full. Share trades are not called again later in the month. Mine trades and debt are not this call. `monthSeed` is month 0, so the day is open, and it writes nothing: no listings, no bids, negative equity so the dividend does not pay, and both people housed so `pinHealth` does not fire. Its journal is still `pay` then `lendBank`.

An empty day on `specialSeed` is not quiet. Ada holds both shares and the equity is 40, so the dividend pays her 40, and Ben has no dwelling, so the engine pins him.

The household shapes are `sellShare`, `buyShare`, `offerHouse`, `buyHouse`, `occupyOrLet`, `keepJob`, `applyJob`, `postRent`, and `applyRent`. `keepJob` and `applyJob` are two shapes. One shape with both tags collapses the decoder. `lots` on a share bid, and `won` on a house bid, are the plan, not interface fields. Job and machine orders may name the listing, or the firm and career, or the builder. The id is minted when the post commits, and the runner binds the single matching listing. Firm posts and machine orders go through `firm:${id}`, a thin leaf, not the household.

`runMutation` writes `rationed` when `filled` is 0 or `filled` is short of `asked`, and `ok` when the order is filled in full. A zero fill is in both logs. A refusal is only in the intent log. Reconciliation already treated `rationed` as landed. The rng is written back before the leaves commit, and only when `stream` changed.

## Rulings

- The allocation is computed once. A leaf that runs first cannot take the unit the plan gave the next buyer. The same rule covers shares, houses, jobs, machines, and rents.
- A full fill is `ok`. A shortfall, including `filled: 0`, is `rationed`. Both commit. A refusal still does not.
- Share trades have no named seller and no second pass. The staple retry is not copied here.
- A house is a lottery, not a price auction. Insolvent bidders and the seller are excluded before the draw. Two solvent bidders advance `stream` by 2.
- The dividend is paid to holders of record after the share book. It does not run on a bank whose equity is not positive. It is not a leaf.
- An incumbent who keeps the chair is not re-checked for education and is not moved onto the posted salary. An applicant is checked even when they already hold the job. Only `keepJob` sets `keep`.
- A chair vacated because its holder won another listing stays empty until next year. Putting the slot back would hire someone into a post the incumbent has already left.
- Inability to pay the posted year's rent is a refusal. A solvent bidder who loses the seat is `rationed`. Cash from earlier on the same day, including a house sale, counts.
- A listed house is kept only by winning the seat. If nobody is seated, the engine vacates the occupant. Then `pinHealth` runs. A person who found a dwelling earlier in the day is not pinned.
- The distress sale of shares and mines is still not this market. `params.distressDiscountPercent` is still unread.
- `salesLastMonth` is not updated. Tax on fills stays where the opening already reads `taxOwed`.
- Mine trades and debt are not this day. Share trades do not run in the ordinary step 7 of a special month.
- The month prefix on `monthSeed` still does not increment `month`.
- `--live` still returns `not implemented`.
