# Milestone 11 — the tech summand

`stay`, `expand`, and `upgrade` post. None of them mints a machine. A generation enters the world when `deliverMachine` builds one. `ops(c, g)` falls in `g`. `output(c, g)` does not. Public supply is that output. Milestones 0–10 are unchanged on `monthSeed`. Its journal is still `pay` then `lendBank`.

## What landed

`strategy` is the effect. The interface's `TechC` shape is not in the `Effect` union, so the posts ride on the effect and the journal tag is `strategy`. The reducer mints listing ids. It does not mint a machine. A tech firm's registry contains `strategy`. Any other kind does not. The mode is handed in. The leaf does not choose it.

The current line is the highest generation this firm has delivered as `builder`. The type is the plurality at that generation. A tie takes the earlier career in `CAREERS`. The volume is `salesLastMonth` when that is a positive integer, and otherwise the number delivered at that generation. A firm that has delivered nothing has no line.

`stay` posts that type, that generation, and that volume, at `params.machinePrice`. `expand` posts `params.expandSlots` engineer seats at that generation, at `params.engineerSalary`. It does not post a machine. `upgrade` posts one engineer seat at the next generation, at `params.advancedSalary`, and one machine of the current type at that generation, quantity 1, at `machinePrice + upgradePremium`. The advanced wage is higher. The new machine is dearer.

`ops(c, 0)` is 2, except a bureaucrat. `ops(c, g)` for `g ≥ 1` is 1. `β` is `ops(bureaucrat, g)`, and it is 1 at every `g`, because 1 is already the floor. A positive integer cannot fall forever. `output(c, 0)` is 20. `output(c, g)` for `g ≥ 1` is 40. The placeholder does not vary by career. The career is still an argument.

Operators are consumed in machine-id order, people in id order. A machine contributes `floor(output · taken / ops)` units, split across the operators who staffed it. One operator on a generation-0 machine produces 10 units, which is one cure and one level. Two operators fill `ops(c, 0)` and produce two awards. A third does not. `awardsPerAssignee` is gone.

A firm with no admin machines is not short of bureaucrats. Admin machines and no bureaucrats scale the productive units to zero. A folded firm and a private firm contribute nothing.

The health seed holds one generation-0 doctor machine and one generation-0 teacher machine, so the public scene still hands out one cure and one level. The builder is not a firm, so the maintenance charge is zero. `runTech` with no order returns before a write.

The stranger scene is `demo-out/tech` on `techSeed`. Mill has delivered one retail machine of generation 0. It upgrades. The offer and the engineer slot are generation 1, and the only machine in the world is still generation 0. The shop then takes delivery. Generation 1 exists, owned by the shop, built by the mill. The shop holds 15. The mill holds 15. Money supply is 30. Interbank is 0. Month is 0. The five rows are zero. Both lines are `agent`, and both reasons are in the intent log.

## Rulings

- The plan is an effect whose posts are frozen by the gate. The reducer does not choose the generation again.
- `stay` prices at `machinePrice`. `upgrade` adds `upgradePremium` and lists one machine, not the current volume. One upgraded product is the prompt's sentence.
- `expand` lists the engineer seats and does not buy. `orderMachine` is still how a firm takes a lot, and the plan has no counterparty to name. `orderMaterial` is not this milestone.
- Absence of `strategy` on a shop is a registry miss: one `refused` line, no `attempted`, no journal. A tech firm that has built nothing is a gate refusal: `attempted`, then `refused`.
- The leaf does not pick `stay`, `expand`, or `upgrade`. That choice, and `upgradeOwn`'s fire or redeploy, is milestone 13.
- `ops` falls once and then holds at 1. `output` rises once and then holds. Sweeping the table is not this milestone.
- Public awards are `floor(units / bundle)` after staffing. Leftover units are not a partial cure. The health seed's machines are what keeps one assignee at one award.
- An admin machine with no bureaucrat produces nothing. No admin machine does not.
- `--live` still returns `not implemented`.
