# Milestone 7 — maintenance, and the fold

Firms meet the opening from their own purse, or they fold. Milestones 0–6 are unchanged on `monthSeed`. The staple market has not been started.

## What landed

`planOpening` walks non-folded firms in id order. The first firm whose cash is short of its bill is folded, and the walk starts again, up to a fixed point. Folding a builder zeros the maintenance the holder owed that builder, so the holder's bill is recomputed before anyone is paid. Survivors then pay. The fold's effects are journaled through `commitEngine`. `folded`, the tax still owed, and the wage lottery's `rng` are a mark applied after those effects. The mark is not a journal line.

`params.maintenance` is 10 for every machine. It is a placeholder, not a per-(career, generation) t₀ vector. `maintenanceCharge` is 0 when the builder firm is missing or `folded`. The pay row is `maintenance`.

The food reserve is `monthsUntilSpecial(month) × params.stapleReservePrice` (5). Month 0 is a special day, so the reserve covers the twelve months until the next one: 60. Otherwise it is `12 − (month % 12)` months. Month 11 reserves one staple, 5. The minimum is one month. The reserve is senior only inside a fold. A solvent firm may spend that cash on the bill.

A solvent firm pays, in order: every wage in person-id order, with no draw on `RngState`; each bank installment as `repayBank` of `min(installment, principal, cash)`, with no interest added and the `bankPrincipal` row still unused; each private installment as a `pay` on row `privateInstallment` that does not shrink principal; tax to `gov`, then the mark reduces `taxOwed` by what was paid; each maintenance pay. A shortfall on a survivor is not how this path pays. The firm was solvent against the whole bill.

A fold draws `min(reserve, cash)` to the owner first, while the firm is still not marked folded, so `drawGate` does not refuse it. Wages then take what remains. If that cash covers every salary, payment is in person-id order and does not touch `RngState`. One worker is paid `min(salary, cash)` with no draw. Two or more workers, and the cash short, are permuted by `nextIndex` without replacement, paid in full while the next salary fits, then a partial if cash remains. Tax is next, then each bank loan: `repay` of `min(principal, installment, cash)`, and `seize` only while that loan still exists, its principal is still positive, and the collateral's lien is that loan. A null lien is read with a ternary. An unmortgaged house is not seized. Each private loan is a partial `pay` up to the installment and the cash, then `writeOff` of the principal still on the books. The holder does not keep the claim. Leftover cash is drawn to the owner, so the firm purse ends at 0. Then the mark sets `folded` and clears `owner.firm` when it pointed here. The firm record stays. Jobs stay until a `fire` shape. A folded firm pays no further wages.

`runMonth` calls `commitOpening` before the government wage. `remainderDraws` still runs after the loan check. It skips a folded firm. `openingPays` is still the helper that pays a full wage bill or nothing; the month does not call it. `monthSeed` has no firm, so its journal is still `pay` then `lendBank`. The purse scene's shop can meet its wage, so that scene's bytes are unchanged.

## Rulings

- The month folds an insolvent firm. It does not leave that cash sitting, and it does not pay a partial wage on the solvent path. `openingPays` still refuses a partial wage. `remainderDraws` still will not draw a firm that has an unpaid bill and too little cash, and a folded firm is skipped.
- A private installment moves a deposit and does not retire principal. The stock ends at `writeOff`. A fold's partial recovery is that pay, then a write-off of whatever principal remains.
- A bank installment is `repayBank` only. No interest is added in this milestone. The row `bankPrincipal` stays empty because that effect's ΔNFA is 0.
- `seize` transfers only the collateral whose lien is that loan. An unmortgaged house stays with its owner.
- The reserve is not a second purse. It is a cap on what the fold may pay creditors, and only then.
- Maintenance to a folded or missing builder is 0. Detection folds one insolvent firm at a time so that zero is visible before survivors pay.
- A loan after the fold does not reach back and pay the wage the fold missed.
- Jobs are not cleared by the fold. The firm record is not deleted. `folded` is not an effect tag.
- The month prefix on `monthSeed` still does not increment `month`.
- `--live` still returns `not implemented`.
- `params.distressDiscountPercent` is named and still not read.
