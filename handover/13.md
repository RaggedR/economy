# Milestone 13 — the other scripted leaves

Four policies, each a pure function of the world, none of them a forecast of the price level. Milestones 0–12 are unchanged on `monthSeed`. Its journal is still `pay` then `lendBank`.

## What landed

`doctorMix` points public doctors at the diseases living people currently hold, one doctor to one disease, highest count first. A tie breaks by `DISEASES` order. `teacherMix` points public teachers at the job archive the same way, by slots, ties by `CAREERS` order. Extra staff are not given an invented specialty. An empty count assigns nobody.

`operatingReserve` is this month's outflow on `maintenance`, `tax`, `bankInterest`, `bankPrincipal`, and `privateInstallment`. `drawAmount` is cash minus that reserve, and never negative. `remainderDraws` uses it. A reserve of zero is the old full draw, so the purse scene is unchanged.

`lenderAccepts` is true when disclosed revenue is at least `params.revenueCover` times the installment. The multiple is 2. A firm's revenue is its positive flows on `staple`, `luxuryFood`, `nightclub`, `tuition`, `privateCure`, and `ore`. The government's revenue is `taxLastMonth`. A decline is not asked. `acceptDebtGate` refuses a holder under `params.savingsThreshold`, which is 100, and otherwise is `issuePrivate`.

`householdSpend` walks one chain, cheapest lot, buyers in id order, one step at a time, skipping a good the purse cannot pay:

- happiness: nightclub
- health: nightclub, luxury food, then one cure of a held disease
- education: nightclub, luxury food, then tuition in the archive career
- savings and investment: nothing

`trainingBids` asks every living person with no job to bid the archive career with the most slots. A person with a job does not bid. `fill` now accepts luxury food and the nightclub as well as staple. Posting those two is still not a command. Tuition and cures already had their own gates.

The month calls the mix, the training bids, and the spend. On `monthSeed` every one of those lists is empty, so nothing new is asked. The lender is not called from the month. There is no debt market to walk.

The stranger scene is `demo-out/leaves` on `leafSeed`. Ada is happiness, Ben is education, Cleo saves. Doc and teach are public staff. The archive lists 3 engineer slots and 1 retail slot. Ada and Ben both hold `d0`. The print is:

- The government reads the diseases people hold when it is asked. The opening draw has already run, and there is no disease ledger. That is the observation the interface names. The plan's "last month" is the ban on a forecast.
- One staff member, one specialty. Staff past the number of positive counts stay unassigned.

- A lender who declines is silent. The savings threshold is a gate, not that silence.
