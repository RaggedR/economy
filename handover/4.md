# Milestone 4 — one mutating leaf

## What landed

`src/bridge/bank.ts`. The bank registry is `post-rate`, `post-material`, and `sell-capital`. Argument scope fences `args.bank` to that leaf's bank. Bank `a` posting bank `b`'s rate is refused with exactly `bank a cannot post bank b's rate`, with no `attempted` line, and the rate is unchanged. The other two commands use `bank a cannot post for bank b`. `postMaterial` and `sellCapital` exist only for mines deposited at that bank.

`src/bridge/household.ts`. The household registry is `request-loan` and `books`. Argument scope refuses a borrower other than that person: `household:ada cannot request a loan for ben`. The same fence as the bank.

`requestLoanGate` judges collateral, LTV, and the leverage ceiling. The bank leaf never sees the decision. LTV is `value * ltvPercent < amount * 100` refused, with `ltvPercent` 50. The lien check is the ternary in `handover/2.md`. A loan of 500 against `h-ada` at bank `a` on `monthSeed` passes. A loan of 501 or 600 against `h-ben` fails LTV. The leaf commits `lendBank` inside the command. The scheduler does not apply it again.

`src/world/policy.ts` is the scripted slot from Part IV. A pure function of the world. The leaf posts the number it returns. The reducer does not compute a second one.

- `nextLoanRate`. One step toward the other bank's posted rate (`rateStep` 1), then one extra step up when `losses > 0` or the book is full. One extra step, even when both are true. On `monthSeed`, bank `a` at 100 moves toward bank `b` at 105 and posts 101. Bank `b` posts 104. With losses, or with a full book, bank `a` posts 102.
- A full book is `ceiling > 0 && loansOutstanding >= ceiling`, with `ceiling = leverageMultiple * markedMineBase`. A marked base of 0 is not a full book, and the rate still takes the single step toward the other bank. `sellCapital` is the other predicate: distressed when `equity < 0` or `loansOutstanding > leverageMultiple * base`. Loans against a zero base are distress for `sellCapital` and are not a full book for the rate.
- `nextOrePrice`. The same step, from the bank's latest listing with `kind: "ore"`. Ore listings carry no material. With no such listing, the current observation is `initialRef[material]`. The other bank posting 12 moves bank `a` from 10 to 11. Optional revenue floor and thin-market haircut stay off.
- `loanAsk`. The borrower's unliened house, or undeposited unliened mine, sorted by id. Amount is `floor(value * ltvPercent / 100)`, installment is that amount, months is 1, and the shape is returned only when `requestLoanGate` would accept it. Otherwise null. On `monthSeed`, ada at bank `a` asks 500 against `h-ada`. The failing LTV scenario is a hand-built shape, not `loanAsk`.

`monthSeed()`, called with deposits at `a` of 700 and deposits at `b` of 400. ada `{a: 400, b: 0}`, ben `{a: 0, b: 400}`, government `{a: 300, b: 0}`. Bank `a` deposit liability −700, equity −700. Bank `b` deposit liability −400, equity −400. Houses `h-ada` and `h-ben`, 10 units each, owners ada and ben, lien null. Mine `m1`, common, stock 100, owner `a`, deposited at `a`. Share `s1`, bank `a`, holder ada, 100 units, `sharesOutstanding` 100. Marked base of `a` is `100 * initialRef.common` = 1000. Ceiling is 10 × 1000 = 10000. House value is 10 × 100 = 1000, so the LTV cap is 500. Government's 300 at `a` can fund the wage of 200. After a loan of 500 the ceiling is still 10000, so ben's 600 fails LTV and does not fail the ceiling.

The bank leaf test posts the policy rate and an ore price. The stranger demo does not have to post a rate.

## Rulings

- The registry is the permission. Households are fenced the same way banks are. A scope refusal has no `attempted` line.
- Commit-inside-the-leaf. The CLI `commit` is `bwdS` for an agent effect. The leaf's `answer` stays synchronous. `tensorC` uses `Promise.all`, and a synchronous `answer` commits the left leaf before the right `b.run` starts, because `await answer()` evaluates `answer()` before the first yield. Do not introduce an await before the commit inside `answer`.
- Policy fills the shape. The leaf posts that rate through the CLI.
- One shared in-process `IdSource` and `Clock` for a demo run. Do not spawn a fresh `seededIds` per CLI invocation.
