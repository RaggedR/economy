# Milestone 10 — disease, the mixes, the public lottery

Disease is drawn with the opening. The government then points its public doctors and teachers. Households bid. The engine's plan serves homeless, then renters, then owners, and lotteries inside a short tier. Private cures and tuition are a later call, after staple and after the special day's rent. Milestones 0–9 are unchanged on `monthSeed`. Its journal is still `pay` then `lendBank`.

## What landed

`pinHealth` stays one effect. The homeless cause is unchanged. The disease cause carries `disease`, because `Person.diseases` and `cure` need the identity and a second tag would only repeat it. A hit appends that disease and sets health to 0. A miss journals nothing. A disease the person already holds journals nothing. Both still advance `rng`.

The chance is `params.diseaseHit` out of `params.diseaseOutOf`. No spec default. The placeholder is 1 of 5. The second draw, only on a hit, picks one of `d0`..`d4` uniformly. `monthSeed` is seed 1, stream 0, Ada then Ben: both miss, and the stream ends at 2.

`assign` writes `Person.assignment`. A doctor at a public hospital is pointed at a disease. A teacher at a public university is pointed at a career. Null clears either. A retail worker, and a doctor at a private hospital, are refused. The mix is handed to the government leaf. It does not choose. That choice is milestone 13.

Public supply is `params.awardsPerAssignee` per living assignee at an open public firm of the right kind. The placeholder is 1. `output(c, g)` is the tech summand, not this market. One award is one cure or one level. A private doctor is not an award.

`planLottery` walks diseases `d0`..`d4`, then careers in `CAREERS` order. Inside a disease or a career the tiers are homeless, renter, owner. A tier the remaining awards cover, and a tier with nothing left, does not draw. A short tier is permuted, one draw per claimant including the last, and the prefix wins. Seed 1, stream 0, homeless Ada then Ben, one award: Ben wins, and the stream advances by 2. An owner behind that tier does not draw. A disease with no public doctor does not draw. A lost bid commits `cure` or `levelUp` with `granted: false` and is `rationed`, in both logs. A bid for a disease the person does not hold is a refusal. A granted cure removes that one disease and does not change health. A granted level adds 1 to that career.

Private cures are not a `Good`, and `fill` stays staple-only. `buyCure` and `buyTuition` are their own effects. Tuition is still a `Good`, and the listing names the career. The provider is named. Two listings for the same provider and disease, or the same career, throw. The plan spends a local cash map, cures then tuition. A bundle shorter than `params.cureSize` (10, fixed) or `params.unitsPerLevel` (10, no spec default) commits qty 0 and is `rationed`. Inability to pay the posted bundle is a refusal. No private step draws.

`runMonth` calls `runDisease` after the wage and `runPublic(ctx, {})` before the loan tensor. Both miss or return before a write on `monthSeed`. `runDiscretionary(ctx, {})` is after `runSpecialDay`. The stranger scene is `demo-out/health` on `healthSeed`, not a full month. Seed 8 strikes Ben with `d2` and misses Ada, Cleo, Doc, and Teach. Cleo already holds `d0`. Doc is pointed at `d2`. Teach is pointed at engineer. Ben is homeless, so he takes the one public cure and his health stays 0. Cleo's `d0` bid is `rationed`. Ada's engineer bid is filled. Cleo's bureaucrat bid is `rationed`. The private hospital posts `d0` at 2. Cleo pays 20. Ben, already cured, is refused, and that refusal is only in the intent log. The private university posts bureaucrat tuition at 1. Cleo pays 10 and reaches level 1. Her purse is 50. The hospital holds 20. The university holds 10. Ada stays at 100. The stream stays 6. The five rows stay at zero. Interbank stays 0. Month stays 0.

## Rulings

- The disease identity is a field of `pinHealth` when the cause is `disease`. The journal tag stays `pinHealth`. There is no second effect.
- The chance lives in `params.ts`. A reducer does not inline it. One in five is a placeholder.
- A miss, and a repeat of a disease already held, advance the stream and write no journal line. Wiring the draw into `runMonth` leaves `monthSeed`'s journal as `pay` then `lendBank`. The stream there is 2.
- The government leaf is asked the mix. It does not read a forecast, and it does not aim at last month's counts. That policy is milestone 13, and it is still open.
- One assigned public worker is one award until `output(c, g)` exists. A folded firm and a private firm contribute nothing. Sweeping `awardsPerAssignee` is not this milestone.
- The lottery is not `productC`. Bids are collected, then one plan draws from `RngState`.
- A full tier, and a tier with no awards left, does not draw. Two homeless claimants and one award advance the stream by 2.
- A public loss commits and is `rationed`. A bid the person cannot legally make is a refusal.
- A cure does not add health. Luxury food is still the only addition to the stock, and it is not this milestone.
- `fill` stays staple-only. A private cure is `buyCure`. Tuition is `buyTuition` even though tuition is a `Good`.
- Inability to pay is a refusal. A short bundle is `rationed` at qty 0. The private book does not draw.
- The stranger scene does not run staple or the special day. `runMonth` is what places the private book after both.
- `--live` still returns `not implemented`.
